Account Deletion Request

We respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data. This page explains how you can request the deletion of your Selm account and what data will be affected.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: Delete from Within the App

  1. Open the Selm application on your device
  2. Go to Settings (tap the gear icon)
  3. Scroll down and tap "Account"
  4. Tap "Delete Account"
  5. Confirm your decision when prompted
  6. Your account will be permanently deleted

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you cannot access the app or prefer to request deletion via email, please send an email to:

Email: selmapp2022@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Include in your email:

  • The email address associated with your Selm account
  • Your full name (as registered in the app)
  • Confirmation that you want to delete your account

We will process your request within 7 business days and send you a confirmation email once completed.

Data That Will Be Deleted

Permanently Deleted Data

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed from our systems:

Data Type Description
Account Information Email address, username, password, profile picture
Profile Data Full name, English proficiency level, learning preferences
Learning Progress Completed lessons, quiz scores, study statistics, achievements
Audio Recordings All pronunciation recordings from speaking exercises
Usage Data App interaction history, learning patterns, session data
Device Tokens Push notification tokens and device identifiers

Data Retention

Data That May Be Retained

Some data may be retained for a limited period after account deletion for the following reasons:

Data Type Retention Period Reason
Transaction Records Up to 7 years Legal and tax compliance requirements
Anonymized Analytics Indefinitely Aggregated, non-identifiable data for service improvement
Support Correspondence Up to 3 years Legal compliance and dispute resolution

Note: Retained data cannot be used to identify you personally after account deletion.

⚠️ Important Notice

  • Account deletion is permanent and irreversible
  • All your learning progress and achievements will be lost
  • You will not be able to recover your account or data after deletion
  • If you have an active subscription, please cancel it before deleting your account
  • You can create a new account anytime, but your previous data cannot be restored

For OAuth Users (Google Sign-In)

If you signed up using Google Sign-In or other OAuth providers:

  • You can still delete your Selm account using the methods above
  • Deleting your Selm account does not affect your Google account
  • To revoke Selm's access to your Google account, visit your Google Account Permissions
